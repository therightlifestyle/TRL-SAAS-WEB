## The Website

This is the official TRL website — a premium, foundation-focused website that communicates:

- **Vision + Reality + Trust**
- "This company is early, but the vision is massive."

The website follows the TRL Framework:
- **Learn** — Knowledge and education
- **Build** — Projects, businesses, ideas
- **Automate** — AI tools and systems
- **Grow** — Community and opportunities

## Sections

1. **Hero** — Main value proposition with "Build Your Future With Intelligence"
2. **Problem** — Ambition Without Systems Creates Frustration
3. **Framework** — The Four Pillars (Learn, Build, Automate, Grow)
