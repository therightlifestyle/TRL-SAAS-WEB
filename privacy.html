Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026

TRL — The Right Lifestyle ("TRL", "we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website, why we collect it, how it is handled, and the choices you have. By using this site, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

Information you give us directly. When you submit the contact form, we receive the fields you fill in: your name, email address, selected subject, and message. We use this only to respond to you and, if you request early access, to keep your place on the early-access list.

Information collected automatically. Our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may log standard technical data such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited, as part of normal server operation. We do not run advertising trackers on this site.

Local storage on your device. The site stores small preferences in your own browser (for example your light/dark theme choice, and any goals you create in the TRL Goals prototype). This data never leaves your device.

2. How We Use Information

  • To reply to your messages and inquiries
  • To maintain and contact the early-access list about TRL launches
  • To understand aggregate usage and improve the website
  • To meet legal obligations where required

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to anyone. Ever.

3. Third-Party Services

  • Formspree — processes contact-form submissions and delivers them to our inbox. Their handling of data is governed by Formspree's own privacy policy.
  • GitHub Pages — hosts this website and serves its files.
  • Google Fonts — delivers the Inter typeface; your browser contacts Google servers to fetch the font files.

4. Data Retention

Contact-form submissions are kept only as long as needed to handle your inquiry and manage the early-access program, after which they may be deleted on request or as part of routine cleanup.

5. Your Rights

You may request at any time to access, correct, export, or delete the personal information we hold about you. To exercise any of these rights, email us — we will respond as quickly as possible.

6. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information we receive. No internet service is perfectly secure, but we limit access to your data and never collect more than the site genuinely needs.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as TRL evolves. The "last updated" date at the top always reflects the current version. Continued use of the site after changes means you accept the updated policy.
